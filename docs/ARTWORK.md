imagegen tool, then resized from the returned 1254-pixel square to the opaque
1024-pixel iOS master. No existing App Store icon or game asset was used as an
input. This is not an official publisher wordmark or an endorsement.

## Final generation prompt

> Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: finished iOS app icon, square 1024 by 1024, full-bleed opaque artwork. Create an original clean Among Us fan-mod icon for the app EHR Among Us: a single large red Among Us crewmate centered, recognizable rounded spacesuit silhouette, backpack, short legs, glossy pale cyan visor, bold charcoal outlines. Deep midnight navy and subtle space-blue background, understated small stars. Make the three letters EHR extremely clear, bold white uppercase, horizontally centered across the middle of the crewmate/icon with a dark navy outline or tight backing for superb small-icon legibility. Text must read exactly EHR, spelled E H R. The letters occupy the center, large enough to read at home-screen size; visor still visibly recognizable above/behind. Polished flat 2D game illustration, crisp edges, restrained highlights, high contrast, simple silhouette. No extra words, no numbers, no official wordmark, no badges, no border, no pre-rounded icon corners, no watermark. Original illustration, do not copy an existing App Store icon. Keep all critical content comfortably within the central 80 percent so iOS corner clipping is safe.
